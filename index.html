India Tax Treatment of 401k / IRA Withdrawals
INCOME TYPE Withdrawals = Income from Other Sources — NOT Capital Gains
401k and IRA withdrawals are not capital assets under Indian law. Capital gains treatment — LTCG/STCG classification, 24-month holding period test, 20% rate with indexation — has no statutory basis under the Income Tax Act for 401k/IRA. The correct and widely-followed approach is Income from Other Sources, taxed at slab rates. This materially changes the India tax calculation and eliminates any LTCG benefit.
TAXABLE BASIS Full Withdrawal Amount Taxable — Conservative Standard Approach
Some advisors argue only the income component (gains above cost basis) should be taxable in India, not the return of principal. This view has no explicit statutory support in the Income Tax Act or any CBDT circular. Most practising CAs treat the full withdrawal as taxable income in India. FTC is then applied against US tax paid. This tool follows the full-withdrawal approach. If you hold a different position, get a written opinion from your CA.
ADVANCE TAX Section 234B — Interest on Large Withdrawals
If your India tax liability in a year exceeds ₹10,000, advance tax payments are mandatory. Large 401k withdrawals can trigger significant 234B interest if not planned. Instalments due: 15 Jun (15%), 15 Sep (45%), 15 Dec (75%), 15 Mar (100%). Plan this before making any withdrawal as ROR — calculate your expected liability early in the year and set up advance tax payments accordingly.
FTC Foreign Tax Credit — Form 67
File Form 67 with your Indian ITR in the withdrawal year — before your ITR submission, not after. FTC is proportionate and capped at your India tax liability on the same income. For most returning NRIs, US federal tax exceeds India slab tax, so net India tax after FTC is close to zero. This is the primary mechanism preventing double taxation for the vast majority of cases.
Section 89A — Tax Deferral Election
WHAT IT IS Section 89A — Defer India Tax to Year of Withdrawal
Introduced by Finance Act 2021. Allows ROR residents to defer India tax on foreign retirement accounts until the US also taxes them — eliminating the annual accrual mismatch. Rule 21AAA prescribes the procedure. Available for accounts in US, UK, and Canada (currently notified countries). Without this election, India can tax annual account growth even if no withdrawal is made.
ONE-TIME DEADLINE Form 10EE — First Year of ROR Only
File Form 10EE online on the Income Tax Portal in your first year of ROR status, on or before your ITR due date. Miss this window = permanently lose Section 89A deferral. Once filed: irrevocable, applies to all notified accounts together, cannot be withdrawn, covers all subsequent years. You cannot selectively apply it to some accounts and not others.
CONDITIONS APPLY Re-emigration Risk Under Rule 21AAA — Conditional, Not Automatic
If you leave India after filing Form 10EE and become a non-resident, all previously deferred income may be taxed in the year you become NR — potentially a large bunched tax bill. Important: this risk applies only if (a) you filed Form 10EE AND (b) the fund is officially notified under Rule 21AAA. It is not triggered automatically for all 401k/IRA holders. Verify notified status with a CA before planning around this risk.
ROTH Section 89A Not Available for Roth Accounts
The deferral election under Section 89A cannot be made for Roth IRA or Roth 401k. India has no provision recognising the Roth tax-free structure — gains may be taxable as income once you are ROR. This is an unsettled area with no CBDT clarity. Do not assume Roth is India-exempt without a professional opinion.
DTAA — India-USA Treaty
ARTICLE 20(2) Pension Relief + Savings Clause Limitation
Under Article 20(2), periodic pension-type payments (e.g. SEPP/72(t) substantially equal periodic payments) may be exclusively taxable in India — meaning the US does not withhold. However, the savings clause (Article 21) blocks this entirely for US citizens and Green Card holders — the US taxes them regardless. For H1B/L1 holders who are not USC/GC, this treaty benefit may apply, but it requires fact-specific professional analysis. FTC remains the practical solution for most returning NRIs.
W-8BEN Dividend Withholding Reduction
File W-8BEN with your US custodian (e.g. Fidelity, Schwab) citing Article 10(2)(b) to reduce dividend withholding from 30% to 25%. If you are an NRA for US purposes, file Form 1040NR to claim a refund on excess withholding. US citizens and GC holders cannot claim NRA status — US tax rules apply in full regardless of India residency.
Black Money Act — Disclosure Requirements
MANDATORY EVERY YEAR Schedule FA / FSI — Annual Foreign Asset Disclosure
Once you become ROR, you must disclose your 401k/IRA balance in Schedule FA every year — even if you make no withdrawal. 401k is not a bank account; the ₹5L bank exemption does not apply. Report any income in Schedule FSI. ITR filing is mandatory as ROR with foreign assets even if your India income is below the taxable threshold — this includes homemakers. Penalty for non-disclosure: ₹10 lakh per year under the Black Money Act.
SEVERE PENALTIES Black Money Act — Burden of Proof on You
India's Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) Act 2015 presumes culpable intent — you must prove non-disclosure was inadvertent, not the other way around. No compounding option exists (unlike FEMA or ITA). If you missed prior years: file a revised return, or an updated return under Section 139(8A) (available up to 24 months from end of assessment year). If that window has also passed, write to your Assessing Officer proactively. Do not ignore missed years.

Glossary

US Retirement Accounts
Traditional 401k / 403b
Employer-sponsored, pre-tax contributions. All withdrawals taxed as ordinary income in the US at applicable rates. 10% early withdrawal penalty before age 59½ (exceptions apply). RMDs start at age 73.
Traditional IRA
Individual Retirement Account with pre-tax contributions. Same US treatment as 401k. 2025 contribution limit: $7,000/year ($8,000 if 50+). RMDs apply at 73. Consider rolling multiple 401k accounts into one IRA before returning — Fidelity supports Indian addresses.
Roth IRA / Roth 401k
After-tax contributions. In the US: qualified withdrawals (age 59½+, 5-year-old account) are completely tax-free. India does not explicitly recognise this structure. Conservative advisors treat Roth gains as Income from Other Sources once you are ROR. Section 89A deferral is not available for Roth accounts.
RMD — Required Minimum Distribution
IRS-mandated annual minimum withdrawals from traditional 401k/IRA starting at age 73 (SECURE 2.0). Calculated as balance ÷ life expectancy factor from IRS tables. Roth IRAs have no RMDs during the owner's lifetime. Missing RMDs = 25% IRS excise tax on the shortfall.
Roth Conversion
Moving funds from a Traditional 401k/IRA to a Roth IRA. Converted amount is ordinary income in the US in the conversion year. During RNOR: India does not tax this foreign income. Optimal strategy: spread over 2–3 RNOR years to stay in lower US brackets.
SEPP / 72(t) — Substantially Equal Periodic Payments
IRS exception allowing penalty-free withdrawals before 59½ using one of three approved actuarial methods. Must continue for 5 years or until age 59½. Relevant for DTAA Article 20(2) analysis — but the savings clause blocks this relief for USC/GC holders.
Indian Tax Concepts
RNOR — Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident
Key benefit: India does NOT tax foreign-sourced income during RNOR. You qualify if NRI in 9 of the 10 preceding FYs, OR if total India stay in the 7 preceding FYs ≤ 729 days. Typically 2–3 years for long-term US residents returning. After RNOR, you become ROR and all global income is taxable in India.
ROR — Resident and Ordinarily Resident
Default resident status after RNOR window expires. India taxes worldwide income. Must file Schedule FA (foreign assets) and Schedule FSI (foreign source income) every year. ITR filing mandatory regardless of India income level if foreign assets are held.
India Tax Treatment of 401k/IRA — Income from Other Sources
401k and IRA withdrawals are taxed as Income from Other Sources at applicable slab rates. Capital gains classification — LTCG/STCG, 24-month holding test, 20% with indexation — is not applicable. There is no statutory basis for capital gains treatment under the Income Tax Act. Full withdrawal amount is the standard taxable basis (not just gains above cost basis, which has no CBDT backing). This materially changes the India tax outcome vs. what some sources incorrectly state.
India New Tax Regime — FY 2025-26 (Budget 2025)
0–4L: 0% · 4–8L: 5% · 8–12L: 10% · 12–16L: 15% · 16–20L: 20% · 20–24L: 25% · >24L: 30%. Section 87A rebate: income ≤ ₹12L → net tax effectively ₹0. 4% Health & Education cess on all tax. 401k/IRA withdrawal is added to other India income and the combined total taxed at slab rates as Income from Other Sources.
Section 89A — Foreign Retirement Account Deferral
Defer India tax on foreign retirement accounts to the year of withdrawal, matching US timing. File Form 10EE in your first year of ROR — one-time, irrevocable election. Applies to all notified accounts together. Re-emigration risk: if you filed Form 10EE and the fund is notified under Rule 21AAA, becoming NR again triggers tax on all deferred income in the NR year. Not available for Roth accounts.
FTC — Foreign Tax Credit (Form 67)
Claim credit for US taxes paid on income also taxable in India. File Form 67 before your ITR submission — cannot be backdated. FTC is proportionate and capped at India tax liability. For most returning NRIs, US federal tax exceeds India slab tax, making net India tax close to zero. This is the primary double-tax prevention mechanism.
Section 234B — Advance Tax Interest
If India tax liability exceeds ₹10,000, advance tax must be paid in instalments. Large 401k withdrawals frequently trigger 234B interest if advance tax is not paid. Dates: 15 Jun (15%), 15 Sep (45%), 15 Dec (75%), 15 Mar (100%). Plan this before making any withdrawal as ROR — not after.
Schedule FA — Foreign Assets Disclosure
Mandatory every year once ROR, even if no withdrawal. 401k is not a bank account — the ₹5L bank exemption does not apply. Penalty for non-disclosure: ₹10 lakh/year under the Black Money Act. Includes homemakers who contributed to 401k while in the US and now have no India income.
US Tax & Legal
US Federal Tax Brackets (2025, Single Filer)
Standard deduction $15,000. Rates: 10% · 12% · 22% · 24% · 32% · 35% · 37%. 401k withdrawals are ordinary income in the US — no capital gains rate applies in either country. US state income tax varies; eliminating state residency before departure removes this layer entirely.
DTAA Article 20 — India-USA Pensions & Savings Clause
Article 20(2): periodic pension payments (SEPP) may be exclusively taxable in India. The savings clause (Article 21) overrides this for US citizens and GC holders — both countries tax regardless of the treaty. For H1B holders not in USC/GC category, treaty relief on periodic payments may be available but requires professional analysis. FTC is the practical tool for the vast majority.
W-8BEN
IRS form to certify non-resident alien status and claim treaty rates with your US custodian. Cite Article 10(2)(b) for dividends — reduces withholding from 30% to 25%. Fidelity, Schwab, and Vanguard all support IRA accounts with Indian addresses. USC and GC holders cannot claim NRA status.
FATCA / CRS
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (US) and Common Reporting Standard (OECD global). US institutions report NRA account holders; Indian institutions report Indian-resident foreign account holders. Your 401k/IRA balance is automatically visible to Indian tax authorities once you are a reportable person. Non-disclosure is not a practical option.
